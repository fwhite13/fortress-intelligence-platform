# What's New in FAIT
**Update: March 7–10, 2026**

Hey team! We've been busy over the weekend and into this week. Here's a quick rundown of what's improved and what's brand new in FAIT — all focused on making your experience smoother and more powerful.

---

## 🗂️ Knowledge Base — Now Properly Separated

The Knowledge Base system has been significantly improved. Each KB type is now fully independent, so your searches stay focused and relevant.

- **Search results stay where they belong** — When you search within a specific KB (e.g., My KB), you'll only see results from that KB. No more accidental bleed-over from other knowledge bases.
- **Four distinct Knowledge Bases:**
  - **Corp KB** — Company-wide documents
  - **My KB** — Your personal documents
  - **Team KB** — Shared documents within your team
  - **Project KB** — Project-specific documents
- **Toggle controls that actually work** — Enable or disable any KB in Settings and your search results will reflect exactly what you've turned on. If you disable Corp KB, those results won't appear. If you enable only My KB, you'll see only your documents.
- **Better document reading** — Uploaded files (PDFs, Word docs, PowerPoints, etc.) are now parsed more accurately, so the AI understands your documents better.

---

## 📎 File Attachments in Chat — NEW

You can now bring documents directly into your conversations.

- **Attach files while you chat** — Use the paperclip (📎) icon in the chat input to upload a file. The AI will read it and you can ask questions about it right in the conversation.
- **Upload confirmation** — After attaching a file, a small chip appears above the input box showing the filename and size so you know it uploaded successfully.
- **Securely stored** — Attachments are stored securely and tied to your conversation.

---

## ✨ Richer AI Responses — NEW

When the AI generates structured content, it now looks polished instead of cluttered.

- **Collapsible panels for rich content** — Code snippets, tables, and formatted documents now appear in clean, collapsible panels with one-click **Copy** and **Download** buttons.
- **Clean output** — No more stray HTML tags or formatting symbols showing up in responses. What you see is what was meant to be shown.

---

## 🎙️ Dictation — Smoother Transcription

- **No more repeated words** — Dictation was occasionally stuttering and duplicating text as you spoke. That's fixed. Speech-to-text now transcribes smoothly from start to finish.

---

## 🔧 Stability Fixes

- **Settings page is stable** — The Settings page was crashing for some users depending on their configuration. It now loads reliably for everyone.

---

## 🏗️ Behind the Scenes

We also made some infrastructure improvements this week for faster, more reliable deployments going forward. Nothing you need to do — just smoother updates for you over time.
