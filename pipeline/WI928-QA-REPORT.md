## What Was Fixed

Close button in the Opportunity Workspace was crashing the Blazor circuit (session disconnect) instead of opening the CloseOpportunityDialog. Root cause: MudSelect nullable enum type mismatch + missing try/catch circuit protection on `CloseOpportunity()`.

Fix applied:
- MudSelect nullable enum type mismatch corrected
- try/catch circuit protection added to `CloseOpportunity()`
