About this proposal

On behalf of Nevada Builders Alliance Insurance Services (NBAIS), we are pleased to present this Workers' Compensation Insurance proposal exclusively for members of the Nevada Builders Alliance (NBA). This proposal has been prepared specifically for your organization and reflects the competitive program rates and enhanced coverage options available through your NBA membership.

NBAIS was established to serve the unique risk management needs of Nevada's construction industry — from residential and commercial builders to specialty trade contractors. As an NBA member, your organization has access to a Workers' Compensation program designed around the realities of your trade, not a one-size-fits-all solution.

Program highlights

  • Exclusive NBA member pricing — competitive group rates unavailable in the open market
  • Construction-class expertise — underwriting specialists who understand your trade
  • Dividend potential — SIG participation with return of premium for favorable loss performance
  • Loss control resources — proactive safety and claims management support
  • Dedicated service team — NBAIS producers with direct carrier access

What is included in this proposal

This proposal package contains the following for your review:

  • Premium Summary & Coverage at a Glance — a summary of your proposed coverage terms and estimated premium.
  • Workers' Compensation Coverage Details — a detailed outline of the proposed coverage terms, limits, and exclusions applicable to your operation.
  • Carrier Quote — the carrier quotation secured for your review, including the class code and payroll basis used to develop this quote. Please review for accuracy and notify us of any changes prior to binding.
  • Coverage Recommendations — a comprehensive list of additional coverage lines for your consideration across commercial, personal, bond, employee benefits, and life planning categories.

Surplus Contribution

As a self-insured group (SIG), BAWNSIG requires a surplus contribution in addition to the estimated premium. This contribution — calculated at 8% of the estimated premium — is a regulatory requirement for SIG participation in Nevada and supports the financial reserves of the group. It is not a fee retained by NBAIS or your producer.
